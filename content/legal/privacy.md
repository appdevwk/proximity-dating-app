# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: September 18, 2026 | Version 2.0**

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Proximity collects, why we collect it, how we use and share it, and the rights you have. Because Proximity is an 18+ adult dating platform, we pay particular attention to age verification, consent records, and location data.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account & Profile Data

- Email address, hashed password, display name, date of birth, gender, preferences, bio, and profile photos.
- Relationship preferences and looking-for information you choose to provide.

### 1.2 Age & Identity Verification Data

- Your explicit 18+ declaration, Terms acceptance (timestamp, version, IP address).
- Verification photos / selfies and derived face fingerprints (biometric embeddings used only for verification and duplicate detection).
- Government-issued identity document images (where you choose or are required to submit them), OCR-extracted date of birth, document type/country, document hash, and face-match scores between the ID portrait and your selfie.
- Liveness / anti-spoof check results.

We minimize retention of raw identity document images and delete or irreversibly anonymize them as soon as reasonably practicable after verification is complete, subject to legal hold requirements.

### 1.3 Location Data

- Approximate or precise location (latitude/longitude or coarse location) when you enable location features for nearby matching.
- You control location permissions through your device settings and in-app controls.

### 1.4 Usage & Technical Data

- Matches, swipes, messages (content and metadata), reports, blocks, and ad interactions.
- Device information, IP address, browser/app type, push notification tokens, and log data.

### 1.5 Payment & Ad Data

- If you use paid features or view ads, transaction records and ad performance data (handled by payment processors and ad partners under their own policies).

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- To operate and improve the Service (matching, messaging, profiles, verification).
- To verify that you are 18 or older and to maintain an auditable record of consent.
- To detect and prevent fraud, scams, underage accounts, duplicate profiles, and prohibited content.
- To enforce our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
- To provide advertising that supports the free tier of the Service.
- To comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests from authorities.
- To communicate with you about the Service, security, and policy updates.

## 3. Legal Bases (Where Applicable)

Where required by law (e.g., GDPR-style regimes), we rely on: contract performance, legitimate interests (safety, fraud prevention, service improvement), consent (where we ask for it, including location and certain verification steps), and legal obligation.

## 4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in these circumstances:

- Service providers that help us operate the platform (hosting, analytics, age-verification vendors, image moderation, customer support) under contractual confidentiality and data-processing terms.
- Ad networks that display ads on the free tier (they receive limited technical and demographic signals, not your full profile).
- Law enforcement or other authorities when required by law or when we have a good-faith belief that disclosure is necessary to protect safety, investigate fraud, or respond to legal process.
- In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate confidentiality protections.
- With your direction or consent.

---

## 5. Age Verification Vendors

When we use a third-party identity or age-verification provider (for example Persona, Veriff, Yoti, Jumio, or similar), that provider processes the documents and biometric data you submit according to its own privacy policy and our data-processing agreement. We instruct such providers to retain raw identity documents only as long as necessary to complete the verification and any required audit trail.

## 6. Cookies, Analytics & Similar Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies for session management, security, analytics, and advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings; some features may not function if cookies are disabled. We enable IP anonymization where supported by analytics providers.

---

## 7. Data Retention

- **Account and profile data:** retained while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward as needed for safety, fraud prevention, and legal compliance.
- **Consent and verification records:** retained for a period sufficient to demonstrate compliance and defend legal claims.
- **Raw government ID images:** minimized; deleted or anonymized promptly after verification unless a longer retention is required by law or ongoing investigation.
- **Messages and media:** retained according to your settings and our operational needs; deleted accounts trigger deletion or anonymization of associated content subject to legal holds.

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time through the in-app controls or by contacting us. Some residual data may remain in backups for a limited period or where retention is required by law.

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have the right to:

- Access the personal information we hold about you.
- Correct inaccurate information.
- Delete your account and personal information (subject to legal exceptions).
- Export a copy of your data.
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
- Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information (as those terms are defined under CCPA/CPRA and similar laws). We do not sell personal information.
- Appeal a decision if we decline a request.

To exercise these rights, use the in-app settings or contact the address published on the Service. We will verify your identity before fulfilling requests.

## 9. Security

We implement technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information, including encryption in transit, hashed passwords, access controls, and private storage of verification materials. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 10. International Transfers

Your information may be processed in the United States and other countries where we or our service providers operate. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) for cross-border transfers.

## 11. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that we have collected information from a person under 18, we will delete it promptly.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be communicated and, where appropriate, we will ask you to accept the revised policy before continuing to use the Service.
