Today marks an important milestone: **Proximity is officially live.**

We set out to solve one of the most persistent problems in online dating — trust. Fake profiles, misrepresented identities, and geographically impractical matches have eroded user confidence in the industry. Proximity addresses this head-on.

### What we built
- **Biometric verification** — real-person confirmation via photo and face matching, with optional government-ID and liveness checks
- **Location-aware matching** — connecting users with verified people in their actual community
- **A safety-first model** — age verification (18+), consent standards, and zero tolerance for commercial sex, aligning with FOSTA-SESTA

### Why it matters
Online dating should feel safe, honest, and local. By making verification the foundation rather than an afterthought, we're building a community people can trust — and we're just getting started.

We're grateful to everyone who helped bring this vision to life, and to the early community members already shaping what Proximity becomes.
